Research

What we work on

Our research focuses on understanding how diet and the gut microbiome interact to influence human health and disease. By combining human studies with mechanistic approaches, we aim to uncover how microbial communities affect immune and gastrointestinal function and translate these discoveries into innovative dietary and microbiome-based therapeutic strategies.
